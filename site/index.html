Pallet: your color collection

Pallet is a color palette collection that themes itself. Browse Kika's palettes, rotate the main color and watch the whole page follow, then copy light and soft-dark CSS.

The free Mac app reads the real palette out of any image or screen area: swatches only, no frame, text or photo. Version 2.0.4, macOS 26 or newer, notarized.
